🛡️ Scam Awareness

Know the common tricks used against small vendors, so your hard-earned money stays safe.

⚠️
Take 2 minutes to read this

Most vendor scams follow the same few patterns. Once you recognize them, they're easy to avoid.

🚩 Common scams to watch for

These are the tricks fraudsters most often use with shop and stall owners.
📷
Fake "Payment Done" screenshots A customer shows a fake UPI/payment success screenshot and walks off before you check your bank app. Always confirm the amount actually landed in your account before handing over goods.
🔳
Switched or pasted-over QR codes Scammers stick their own QR code sticker over yours so payments go to them instead of you. Check your QR code regularly against the one you originally printed.
💸
"Scan to receive money" trick You never need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money — only to send it. Anyone asking you to "scan to get paid" is trying to take money from you, not give it.
☎️
Fake bank or KYC calls Callers pretending to be from your bank or a payment app ask for OTPs, PINs, or app permissions to "verify" or "reactivate" your account. Banks never ask for your OTP or PIN over the phone.
📦
Fake bulk-order suppliers A "wholesaler" offers stock at unusually low prices and asks for advance payment, then disappears. Verify suppliers before paying in advance, especially ones contacted only online.
💵
Counterfeit or altered currency notes Fraudsters pay with fake or altered notes during busy hours when you're distracted. Check large notes carefully, especially during rush periods.

✅ How to protect yourself

Simple habits that stop most of these scams before they start.
🔊
Turn on payment voice confirmation Enable the audio alert in your payment app so you hear the amount received in real time, instead of relying on a customer's screen.
🔍
Check your QR code weekly Scan your own displayed QR code once in a while to confirm it still points to your account.
🔒
Never share OTP, PIN or app screen No genuine bank, payment app, or government scheme will ever ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, or ask you to share your screen.
🧾
Keep a paper backup of daily totals A quick handwritten tally alongside your app records makes it easy to spot a missing or mismatched payment the same day.
👥
Verify new suppliers before advance payment Ask other vendors if they've dealt with a supplier before, or meet in person, before sending money upfront.

📞 If you think you've been scammed

Report it immediately — acting fast improves the chance of recovering your money.
